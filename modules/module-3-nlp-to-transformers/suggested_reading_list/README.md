# Suggested reading: Module 3 (NLP to Transformers)

Module 3 is the conceptual peak: tokens, attention, and the transformer. These are the guides that make it click, and the paper that started it. All are free.

## Blogs and articles

- [The Illustrated Transformer, Jay Alammar](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/) - the canonical visual walk-through of attention and the block.
- [The Illustrated Word2vec, Jay Alammar](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-word2vec/) - "meaning as a vector", drawn out.
- [The Annotated Transformer, Harvard NLP](http://nlp.seas.harvard.edu/annotated-transformer/) - the original paper, line by line, as runnable code.
- [Attention? Attention!, Lilian Weng](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2018-06-24-attention/) - a deeper survey of attention once the basics land.

## Videos

- [Neural Networks: Zero to Hero, Andrej Karpathy](https://karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html) - build a GPT from scratch, in code, from the ground up.
- [Neural networks series, 3Blue1Brown](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks) - the later chapters give attention and transformers the visual treatment.

## Course

- [Hugging Face LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1) - free and hands-on with real transformer models.

## The paper

- [Attention Is All You Need, Vaswani et al. (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) - the transformer, in its original form.
